Marginal-yield pilot and Top-K chart
==================================

Purpose
-------
Choose a review-budget-efficient semantic retrieval depth, using discovery of
distinct investigative leads rather than score thresholds or presumed labels.
The plotted data are actual initial analyst assessments, not the hypothetical
numbers in the proposed methodology.

Sampling and denominators
-------------------------
The complete ranks were fixed from all 152,885 saved score rows. Ties were
ordered by email_id. For each pattern, 40 emails were drawn without replacement
from each band: 1-100, 101-200, 201-300, and 301-500, using seed 6450.
This gives 480 pattern-band slots and 431 unique sampled emails. There are
419 distinct saved retrieval strings; repeated text is retained in the email
denominator because each sampled email is a retrieval/review opportunity.

Marginal yield = newly identified distinct credible investigative leads /
additional unique emails actually assessed in that interval.

Each per-pattern interval has denominator 40. An email is never repeated within
one pattern's disjoint bands, but it can occur under more than one pattern.
Do not sum those denominators for combined email-review cost. The combined
chart deduplicates emails at the earliest sampled stage: 112, 110, 107, 102.
Cases are counted once globally and separately once per pattern in its own
diagnostic curve. Raptor's WHO and HOW MUCH hits are one global umbrella case.
Mahonia was known before this pilot and is not credited as a new discovery.

The pilot used the original population, with no added date cutoff or keyword
filter. This preserves comparability with the three-query baseline. Some
correspondence is post-October 16, 2001; a pre-cutoff-only design would require
new ranks, sampling and a separate analysis.

Assessment scope and rubric
---------------------------
Reviewer: Codex. Initial retrieval-text triage used abbreviated excerpts for
long messages, followed by targeted full-message checks on escalated cases.
This is not a fully adjudicated human-labeled sample. The audit HTML includes
all saved retrieval text and full cleaned context, plus per-pattern judgments.
Unclear items and contextual messages are not established negatives.

Credible investigative lead means a named transaction/product with a concrete
retained-risk, protection-capacity or representation issue worth tracing.
It is not a conclusion that wrongdoing occurred and need not yet prove all
three evidence conditions. Routine drafting, policy notices, secondhand news,
entity names and lawful mitigation by themselves do not qualify.

The lead registry explains why each counted case was retained and preserves
the legitimate alternatives. Case grouping is subjective; a stricter rubric
or treating each Raptor series separately would change the numbers. The source
scores and random sample are unchanged so another reviewer can adjudicate them.

HOW MUCH's final sampled band contains the Raptor/NewPower assurance and

Two dating concerns are retained as unresolved: a November swap-to-forward

Other specific disclosure/compliance concerns, including California exposure
statements, EOTT sale-linked earnings, and historical GAAP representations in

were not automatically counted as the three specified patterns.

K decision
----------
A shared stopping depth is NOT demonstrated through K=500. A naive two-zero-
band rule would have suggested stopping HOW MUCH at K=300, yet the look-ahead
301-500 band discovered two leads (5%). This is an actual example of the
proposed method's warning that a low-yield interval can precede discoveries.

Use K=500 as a provisional working pool if a depth must be chosen now:
the union of the three top-500 lists contains 1,141 unique emails. This is a
coverage/review-budget choice, not a demonstrated optimum. Before treating it
as final, extend HOW MUCH beyond 500 and resolve the flagged context/dating
items. WHO has two observed low-yield increments ending at 500; WHEN's low
yield should trigger query-quality review and targeted date-context checks,
not a claim that the pattern is absent.

The full nested pool sizes are:
K=100: 256 unique emails
K=200: 494 unique emails (238 additional)
K=300: 715 unique emails (221 additional)
K=500: 1,141 unique emails (426 additional)

Uncertainty
-----------
With 40 sampled emails, one new case changes the observed yield by 2.5
percentage points. A zero cannot certify population yield below 1%.
Sampling changes discovery probabilities for repeated cases and correlated
threads. Do not extrapolate distinct cases by multiplying sampled yield by
band size, and do not treat ordinary binomial intervals as confidence limits
for distinct-case discovery yield.

The 1% dashed line is illustrative and operational, not statistically optimal.
Combined rates are below 1% at all tested depths but hide the late HOW MUCH
increase. Always inspect each pattern before selecting a shared depth.
